Sweep — Support

A to-do app you clear with your hands, plus the everyday things that used to live in seven other apps.

Get in touch

Write to support@kaliwa.dev. There is a Contact support row in the app under Settings that opens a message with your version and device already filled in, which is the quickest way to get a useful answer.

Sweep records no analytics of any kind. Nothing reports which screens are used or where anyone gives up, so a message really is the only way a problem reaches us.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? On your iPhone, in one file. There is no account and no server.
  • How do I move it? Settings, then Export a backup. It is a single JSON file you can keep anywhere.
  • I deleted something by mistake. Shake the phone to undo. Anything removed also waits 30 days in the Archive.
  • I paid and Pro is not on. Settings, then Restore purchases. If that does not do it, write to us with the date of the purchase.
  • Does it need Apple Health? No. Health is optional, off by default, and every feature works without it.
  • Can I cancel? Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings app, your name, Subscriptions.

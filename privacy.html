Sweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Sweep is a to-do and time tracking app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This page explains exactly what the app does with your information. The short version: your notes, tasks, timers and settings stay on your device, and there is no Sweep account to create.

What stays on your device

Everything you type into Sweep is written to a file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone:

  • Lists, tasks, reminders and archived items
  • Countdowns and their alerts
  • Routine templates, each day's plan and what you marked on it
  • Pomodoro history and tracked work sessions
  • Subscriptions you record, including names, amounts and notes
  • Water you log and the daily goal you set
  • Weight readings you enter, and the goal and trend worked out from them
  • App settings such as theme, sound set and haptics

None of this is sent to us. We do not operate a server that receives your content, and we cannot read it. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it. You can export a copy at any time from Settings.

Reminders and alerts

Reminders are delivered by iOS as local notifications, scheduled on the device itself. No reminder text leaves your phone.

Apple Watch

If you use the Watch app, your daily routine is copied to the Watch directly from your iPhone using Apple's device-to-device connection, and the Watch face complication reads it from the Watch's own storage. The data does not pass through us. iPhone and iPad do not share data with each other; a backup file you export yourself is the only way it moves between them.

Apple Health

Sweep can exchange two kinds of information with Apple Health. Both are switched off until you turn them on, and every feature of the app works with them left off.

  • Water. When you turn this on, each drink you log is also added to Apple Health. Sweep only writes; it never reads water back, so a glass recorded in another app cannot appear twice.
  • Weight. You choose the direction. Sweep can add the weights you enter to Apple Health, read the ones your scale or another app has written, or both. Reading lets a reading from a smart scale show up in Sweep without you typing it again.

This information is handed to Apple Health on your device and read back from it on your device. It is not sent to us, it is not sent to Adapty, and it is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can withdraw either permission at any time in the Health app, under Sharing, and in Sweep's own settings.

Purchases

Sweep is free to use. Sweep Pro is an optional paid upgrade. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your card details or your Apple Account.

To know whether your Pro access is active, the app uses Adapty, a subscription management service. Adapty receives:

  • Your purchase and subscription history for this app
  • A device identifier that Apple provides to us, plus basic device information such as model, operating system version, language and region
  • App version and the moments you view or dismiss the upgrade screen

Advertising identifiers are switched off in our configuration, and Sweep does not track you across other apps or websites. If Adapty cannot be reached, the app falls back to the last known state stored on your device, so it keeps working offline.

What Sweep never collects

  • Your name, email address or phone number
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos or browsing history
  • The contents of your tasks, notes or timers
  • The water and weight you record, or anything read from Apple Health. That information stays on your device, as described above.

There are no advertisements in Sweep, and no data is sold or shared for advertising.

Children

Sweep is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove every piece of data it stores on your device.
  • Export a backup from Settings before deleting, if you want to keep it.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in the Settings app on your iPhone, under your Apple Account.
  • To ask about the purchase records held by Adapty, contact us at the address below.
